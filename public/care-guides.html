Vaccination Protocols & Due Dates

Core vaccines protect dogs and cats against life-threatening viral pathogens. Staying on schedule prevents gaps in immunity.

  • Canine Rabies: Legally required booster given every 1 to 3 years.
  • Canine DHPP / DA2PP: Protects against Distemper, Hepatitis, Parvovirus, and Parainfluenza.
  • Feline FVRCP: Core 3-in-1 protecting against Rhinotracheitis, Calicivirus, and Panleukopenia.
  • Lifestyle Vaccines: Bordetella (kennel cough) and Leptospirosis based on boarding and outdoor risk.

Smart QR Passports vs Microchips

Microchips require veterinary scanners, but smartphone QR tags allow anyone who finds your pet to access vital emergency details instantly.

  • Zero App Required: Any modern smartphone camera scans the collar QR tag.
  • Owner-Controlled Privacy: You choose whether your phone number or allergies are visible.
  • One-Click Revocation: Regenerate identifiers instantly if a tag is misplaced.
  • Medical Alerts: Critical alerts (e.g. diabetic or seizure precautions) display immediately.

Dangerous Household Foods

Keep common human foods away from curious companion animals to avoid life-threatening toxicity.

  • Xylitol / Birch Bark Sweetener: Present in sugar-free gums and nut butters; causes severe hypoglycemia and liver failure in dogs.
  • Chocolate & Caffeine: Theobromine toxicity causes cardiac arrhythmia and tremors.
  • Grapes & Raisins: Can induce sudden acute renal failure in canines.
  • Alliums (Onions & Garlic): Causes hemolytic red blood cell destruction.

Preparing for Veterinary Visits

Maximize the value of your annual exam with structured medical preparation.

  • Bring your PetCare Passport timeline showing past vaccination dates and batch records.
  • Note subtle behavioral changes: water intake, appetite, mobility after resting.
  • List active supplements and dosages currently administered at home.
  • Use carriers for cats and secure leashes in veterinary clinic reception lobbies.
